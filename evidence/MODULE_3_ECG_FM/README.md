## 1. Module Purpose

Module 3 extracts standardized, high-dimensional latent representations from the degraded ECG waveforms generated by Module 2 using the official pretrained ECG-FM foundation model (McKeen et al., *JAMIA Open*, 2025). By freezing the 90.88M parameter wav2vec 2.0 backbone, Module 3 produces an invariant, benchmarked embedding space ($\mathbb{R}^{768}$) that isolates the intrinsic physiological representation capacity of the foundation model across all seven lead configurations.

---

## 2. Scientific Question Addressed

> *How are self-supervised foundation model representations deformed when physiological observation leads are progressively removed, and how does latent manifold drift correspond to the anatomical plane of the missing channels?*

Rather than training diagnostic classifiers directly on raw time-series, foundation models compress temporal and inter-lead dependencies into high-level latent concepts. Module 3 measures the geometric stability of these concepts under lead loss.

## 4. Key Processes and Design Rationale

### 4.1 Strict Backbone Parameter Freezing
- **Implementation:** The `Wav2Vec2CMSCModel` architecture was loaded with `requires_grad = False` for all 90,883,072 parameters and executed strictly under `torch.no_grad()`.
- **Rationale:** Preserving the pretrained weights prevents task-specific adaptation or lead-specific overfitting. Any observed change in representation vector $\mathbf{z}$ is strictly an empirical property of the pretrained model's response to lead degradation.

### 4.2 Temporal Context Pooling
- **Implementation:** Contextual sequence states $\mathbf{X}_{\text{seq}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 62 \times 768}$ produced by the 12 Transformer layers are averaged over the sequence length ($T=62$):
  $$\mathbf{z}_{\text{ECG}} = \frac{1}{62} \sum_{t=1}^{62} \mathbf{x}_{\text{seq}}[t]$$
- **Rationale:** Produces a unified vector $\mathbb{R}^{768}$ that captures the global electrophysiological state of the 10-second recording for downstream classification, clustering, and drift analysis.

### 4.3 Zero-Mask Forward Pass Compatibility
Degraded leads are presented as exact 0.000 mV channels within the standard 12-channel input layer. The network processes the input through its native 1D convolutions without requiring ad-hoc input channel pruning.

## 7. Practical and Research Applications

1. **Rapid Downstream Multi-Task Probing:** Enables rapid training of diagnostic classifiers, linear probes, and multi-modal attention adapters without backpropagating through 90M parameters.
2. **Geometric Latent Space Analysis:** Enables researchers to analyze how deep representations degrade in high-dimensional space under sensory loss.
3. **Evaluation of Lead Information Value:** Demonstrates that spatial orientation (frontal vs horizontal plane) impacts foundation model embeddings more heavily than raw channel count.

---

## 8. Known Defects and Methodological Limitations

1. **Temporal Averaging:** Mean pooling over 62 frames discards fine-grained beat-by-beat temporal timing and paroxysmal event localization.
2. **Drift as an Imperfect Diagnostic Proxy:** High cosine similarity (>0.95) does not guarantee that critical diagnostic decision boundaries have not been crossed.
3. **Out-of-Distribution Zeroing:** The foundation model's GroupNorm layers were not pretrained on flatline zeroed channels, potentially introducing out-of-distribution activation shifts.
4. **Pretraining Demographic Imbalance:** Pretraining on the MIMIC-IV-ECG ICU population may over-index representations on inpatient cardiac comorbidity patterns.

*Detailed analysis:* [module3_limitations.md](file:///e:/ECG/evidence/MODULE_3_ECG_FM/limitations/module3_limitations.md).

---

## 9. Evidence Gaps

1. **Attention Map Extraction:** Frame-level cross-attention maps between leads were not exported; only pooled latent vectors were cached.
2. **Comparison with Alternative Foundation Models:** Representations were extracted only from ECG-FM; comparative extraction from other foundation models (e.g., HuBERT-ECG, ST-MEM) was not performed.
