Problem Statement & Measurable Objectives

The Clinical Safety Gap

  • Clinical Reality: Emergency triage, ambulances, and ICU telemetry rarely have static, complete 12 leads; electrodes detach or only 1–2 leads are recorded.
  • The Metric Mirage: Deep models preserve deceptively high diagnostic accuracy (0.95 Macro-AUROC) by memorizing dataset shortcuts.
  • Hallucinatory Reasoning: Generative models emit clinical explanations citing unobserved leads (e.g., diagnosing Anterior Infarction by citing V1–V4 ST-elevation when only limb leads exist!).

Four Measurable Objectives

  • Objective 1: Formalize the Reasoning Failure Boundary (RFB): the exact lead threshold below which clinical grounding collapses.
  • Objective 2: Formulate the Area Under the Reasoning Degradation Curve (AURDC) to quantify resilience across 7 lead levels (12 → 1).
  • Objective 3: Build a Dual-Path Reasoning Transformer comparing unaugmented ECG reasoning against SNOMED-CT Clinical Knowledge Priors.
  • Objective 4: Deploy an auditable Ternary Grounding Gate classifying claims as SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, or UNAVAILABLE.

Related Work & The Identified Research Gap

Existing Foundation Models & Benchmarks

  • PTB-XL Benchmark (Strodthoff et al., 2021): Standardized 12-lead deep learning baseline; restricted exclusively to label correctness.
  • ECG-FM (McKeen et al., 2025): 90.8M-parameter wav2vec2-CMSC foundation model; high-quality latent vectors but zero evidence grounding.
  • GEM & Multimodal ECG-LLMs (Lan et al., NeurIPS 2025): Employs generative waveform inpainting to reconstruct missing leads—dangerous in clinical cardiology.

The Core Research Gap Addressed

"Prior work either benchmarks exclusively on fixed 12-lead setups, or synthesizes unmeasured waveforms through generative hallucination. No existing framework audits whether clinical reasoning remains truthfully grounded in physical sensor availability under progressive lead deprivation."
  • Zero Inpainting: Strictly evaluates observable physical electrodynamic vectors.
  • Provenance Tracking: Cabrera DAG tracking ensures exact physical vector lineage.

Proposed Methodology: 6-Phase Closed-Loop Pipeline

The 6 Auditable Engineering Phases

  • Phase 1: Multi-Cohort Harmonization: 36,957 records filtered via zero-phase Butterworth (0.5–40 Hz), 100 Hz resampling.
  • Phase 2: Controlled Degradation Engine: 7 lead levels ($12 \to 10 \to 8 \to 6 \to 4 \to 2 \to 1$) tracked by Cabrera DAG.
  • Phase 3: Dual Feature Extraction: Frozen 768-D $Z_{\text{ECG}}$ latents + discrete rule-extracted observations $O_l$.
  • Phase 4: Dual-Path Reasoning Transformer: Control Path (ECG-only) vs Experimental Path (SNOMED-CT CKP).
  • Phase 5: Deterministic Grounding Gate: Expert-ratified ternary logic (SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, UNAVAILABLE).
  • Phase 6: Degradation Compilation: Quantifying Evidence Observability, GQ, AURDC, and the RFB.

Empirical Results & Comparative Performance Analysis

Key Findings across 51,625 Test Evaluations

  • The Accuracy vs Observability Paradox:
    AUROC remains virtually flat from 12 leads (0.9540) down to 2 leads (0.9487), while Evidence Observability collapses from 100% to 41.9%.
  • Anatomical Non-Monotonicity:
    4-lead (0.9524 AUROC) outperforms 6-lead (0.9479 AUROC) because 4-lead retains electrode V1.
  • Reasoning Failure Boundary Delay:
    Control crosses safety threshold at 4 leads (RFB = 4). CKP preserves grounding to 2 leads (RFB = 2).
  • AURDC Gain: Improves from 0.70 to 0.81 (+15.7% relative improvement).
